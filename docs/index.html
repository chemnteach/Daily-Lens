FAA Ground Stop Grounds Thousands of Flights Nationwide
A nationwide FAA ground stop has resulted in thousands of flight cancellations as airlines work to restore normal operations. The FAA administrator attributed the disruption to equipment failure. This marks the first such ground stop since January 2023, when a similar FAA computer system malfunction occurred, and only the second nationwide ground stop since the September 11, 2001 attacks. Airlines are actively working to resume flights and recover from the operational disruption.
