El Niño has officially formed in the Pacific Ocean, with US meteorologists forecasting it could reach historic strength levels by fall, potentially rivaling or exceeding the record 1997 event. The Climate Prediction Center announced Thursday that this year's El Niño is shaping up to be among the strongest on record. Scientists warn the phenomenon, which influences weather patterns globally, will likely intensify extreme weather conditions worldwide and further contribute to planetary heating. The climate pattern is expected to impact weather across multiple regions as it develops through the coming months.
