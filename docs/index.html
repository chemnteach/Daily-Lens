Senate Democrats Block Datacenter Energy Cost Bill
Senate Democrats blocked the Ratepayer Protection Act on Wednesday, legislation designed to address rising electricity costs linked to data center expansion. The bill, which passed the House with bipartisan support, aimed to shift the burden of increased energy consumption away from consumer electricity bills. Democrats opposed the measure, characterizing it as ineffective or "toothless" in addressing the issue. Tennessee execution of Christa Pike halted hours before scheduled
Tennessee's first female execution in over 200 years was halted by a federal appeals court on Wednesday, just an hour before it was scheduled to take place. Christa Pike, convicted of murder committed at age 18, had her execution stayed by the Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals in a 2-1 split decision. Both the state governor and Tennessee Supreme Court had previously refused clemency requests. The split court decision leaves the case unresolved, with sources noting Pike's status as a historic case that was prevented from proceeding at the final hour.
