Federal prosecutors seek to dismiss Breonna Taylor warrant charges
Federal prosecutors have moved to dismiss charges against two former Louisville police officers accused of falsifying the warrant used in the 2020 raid that resulted in Breonna Taylor's death. Judges had twice reduced the felony charges to misdemeanors, finding no direct legal link between the false warrant information and Taylor's death. The Department of Justice's decision to drop the cases comes after these judicial rulings limited the charges' viability. Taylor, a Black woman, was fatally shot during the controversial raid at her apartment.

Federal Judge Blocks Pentagon Press Restrictions as Unconstitutional
A federal judge has ruled that the Pentagon's new press access policy violates the First Amendment, siding with The New York Times in a constitutional challenge. The policy, implemented under Defense Secretary Hegseth, drew criticism from news organizations alleging it gave the Department of Defense excessive authority to punish reporters and outlets over unfavorable coverage. While the court acknowledged the importance of protecting national security and military operations, it determined that the restrictions on press access overstepped constitutional bounds. The ruling blocks key portions of the policy, marking a significant victory for press freedom advocates concerned about government restrictions on journalism.

Trump Administration Lifts Sanctions on Iranian Oil
The Trump administration has temporarily waived sanctions on Iranian oil stranded at sea for 30 days in an effort to address rising global energy prices amid ongoing U.S.-Iran tensions. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent stated the move would bring approximately 140 million barrels to market. While the administration characterizes the measure as narrowly tailored and short-term, sources differ in emphasis: some highlight the connection to the U.S.-Israeli military conflict with Iran, while others focus primarily on the energy cost implications. The authorization permits the sale of previously restricted Iranian oil, marking a significant policy shift despite administration reassurances that Tehran will not substantially benefit from the arrangement.
