Supreme Court Limits Gun Prosecutions for Marijuana Users
The Supreme Court unanimously ruled that federal gun laws cannot be broadly applied to prosecute all illegal drug users, deciding in favor of a Texas man charged with firearm possession despite regular marijuana use. Justice Neil Gorsuch wrote that such prosecutions violate Second Amendment protections, rejecting the government's comparison to historical disarmament of habitual drunkards in the founding era. The decision limits the scope of federal gun statutes while affirming Second Amendment rights. Right-leaning outlets highlighted the ruling's connection to the Hunter Biden prosecution, while all sources agree on the unanimous nature of the decision and its significance for gun law interpretation.
