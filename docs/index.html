Supreme Court Weakens Voting Rights Act Protections
The U.S. Supreme Court issued a 6-3 ruling striking down provisions of the Voting Rights Act, a decision that has sparked significant political backlash. The majority's decision appears based on the premise that America has sufficiently addressed racial discrimination in voting. Critics, including House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries, argue the ruling ignores ongoing voting rights concerns, prompting President Trump to call for Jeffries's impeachment. The ruling is intensifying state-level redistricting battles and raising questions about the adequacy of existing voting protections moving forward.
