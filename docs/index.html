SpaceX Goes Public in Record-Breaking IPO
SpaceX, Elon Musk's aerospace company, has completed its initial public offering, becoming the largest IPO in stock market history. The company's shares began trading on the Nasdaq at $150 per share, valuing SpaceX at approximately $1.7 to $2 trillion. The offering is expected to raise roughly $75 billion and has made Musk the world's first trillionaire based on his ownership stake. The company operates spacecraft, satellite communications, and artificial intelligence businesses. Sources across the spectrum focus on the historic scale of the IPO and its wealth implications for Musk, while noting the company's diverse revenue streams.

Federal Judge Blocks Trump Administration's $1.8B Settlement Fund
A federal judge has issued an indefinite block on the Trump administration's $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization" fund, which was created to resolve a lawsuit against the IRS over the leak of the former president's tax returns. U.S. District Judge Leonie Brinkema extended a temporary injunction into a permanent one during a Friday hearing, preventing the Justice Department from making any payments from the fund. The decision halts what the administration characterized as compensation for alleged government overreach, while critics questioned the fund's purpose and legality.
