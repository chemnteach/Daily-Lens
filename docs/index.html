Topic 03 of 05
Debate Over Birthright Citizenship Threatens Education Access
A growing political debate centers on whether children born to undocumented immigrants in the U.S. should receive birthright citizenship and maintain access to free public K-12 education. Currently, the Supreme Court's Plyler v. Doe decision guarantees all children, regardless of immigration status, the right to free public education. Republican lawmakers are increasingly calling to overturn this precedent, with discussions expanding from state legislatures to the White House. Education advocates warn that eliminating birthright citizenship could complicate school access and college enrollment, while supporters of the change argue it warrants reconsideration as part of broader immigration policy reform.
