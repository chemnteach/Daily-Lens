US Loses Measles Elimination Status Amid Outbreak
The United States has officially lost its measles elimination status after maintaining it for 26 years since 2000, according to health authorities. The country is experiencing its worst measles outbreak since 1991, with over 5,400 cases recorded, indicating the disease is now circulating continuously within the US population. This marks a significant public health milestone, as measles elimination—meaning the disease is no longer endemic—is now gone. The announcement comes as health officials assess vaccination rates and transmission patterns across the country.
