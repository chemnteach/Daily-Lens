Blanche Attorney General Nomination Advances to Senate Vote

The advancement comes after Blanche reached a deal with Republican holdouts, particularly Senator John Cornyn, regarding a $1.776 billion "anti-weaponization" fund that had stalled the nomination. While left-leaning outlets note concerns from some lawmakers about Blanche's background as Trump's former personal lawyer, right-leaning sources present the committee vote as a straightforward procedural advancement. The deal's resolution of GOP reservations appears to have cleared the path for the nomination to proceed to the broader chamber.
