Trump Pushes Voting Citizenship Requirement Bill
The SAVE America Act, a Republican-backed voting requirement bill championed by President Trump as a top legislative priority, mandates that voters prove citizenship with specific documents before registration. Democrats, including Rep. James Clyburn, oppose the measure, arguing it resembles Jim Crow-era restrictions and would disenfranchise an estimated 21 million citizens lacking required documentation. Trump has pledged to oppose any politician voting against the bill. The Senate is debating the legislation this week, with disagreement centered on whether the citizenship verification requirement protects election integrity or imposes unreasonable barriers to voting access.
