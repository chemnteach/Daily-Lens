Supreme Court Blocks Trump's Firing of Federal Reserve Governor
The Supreme Court issued a 5-4 decision blocking President Trump's attempt to remove Federal Reserve board member Lisa Cook from her position, marking the first time a president has sought to fire a Fed governor. The ruling allows Cook to remain in her role while her legal challenge proceeds through the courts. Supreme Court Upholds Mail-In Ballot Grace Periods
The U.S. Supreme Court ruled that mail-in ballots may be counted if they arrive after Election Day, rejecting Republican efforts to restrict mail voting procedures. The decision represents a setback for President Trump's administration, which had opposed extended ballot counting periods. The ruling comes as the Court has issued mixed decisions this term on voting-related matters, simultaneously expanding presidential power in other regulatory areas. The decision affects states that currently allow grace periods for mail-in ballot receipt and reflects ongoing judicial disagreement over voting access policies.

Supreme Court Rejects Trump's Appeal in Carroll Case
The Supreme Court has rejected President Trump's appeal of a $5 million jury verdict in a defamation case brought by writer E. Jean Carroll. The decision, which the Court declined to review, means Trump must now pay the damages awarded in the 2022 case. Carroll had accused Trump of sexual abuse, and the jury found in her favor on defamation claims. The Supreme Court's rejection of the appeal effectively upholds the lower court's decision without further legal challenge at the nation's highest court.
