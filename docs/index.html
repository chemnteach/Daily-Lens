NASA Launches Artemis II Moon Mission With Four Astronauts
NASA has launched Artemis II from Kennedy Space Center, marking the first crewed mission toward the moon in over 50 years. The four-person crew includes NASA astronauts Victor Glover, Christina Hammock Koch, and Reid Wiseman, along with Canadian Space Agency astronaut Jeremy Hansen. Coverage focuses on both the historic significance of returning Americans to lunar exploration and introducing the international crew selected for this pivotal mission.

Supreme Court Reexamines Birthright Citizenship Policy
The Supreme Court is hearing arguments in a case challenging birthright citizenship, a long-standing immigration policy rooted in the 14th Amendment and upheld by the Court over a century ago. President Trump signed an executive order on his first day back in office seeking to end the practice. The case represents a significant reexamination of whether children born in the United States to non-citizen parents automatically receive citizenship, a foundational principle that has shaped American immigration law for generations.
