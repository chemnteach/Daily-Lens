Met Museum Cancels Planned John Galliano Fashion Exhibition
The Metropolitan Museum of Art has canceled a planned 2027 exhibition retrospective dedicated to renowned fashion designer John Galliano following widespread backlash. The decision stems from Galliano's past antisemitic and racist comments made in 2010 and 2011, which surfaced years later and led to his conviction in an antisemitism trial in France. Galliano was previously fired from his position as creative director of Christian Dior due to these controversial statements. The announcement of the Met Gala exhibition prompted significant public opposition, ultimately leading the institution to withdraw the planned tribute.

The House of Representatives returned Monday from a five-week August recess to address critical legislative priorities, particularly a stopgap spending bill needed to prevent a government shutdown.
