FBI Investigates Suspicious Letters Sent to US Officials

Trump Cancels Plan to Livestream Fort Hood Shooter Execution
President Trump has decided to cancel a plan to livestream the execution of Nidal Malik Hasan, the former Army major convicted of killing 13 people in the 2009 Fort Hood shooting. The livestreaming proposal, which originated from Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, faced widespread international criticism and condemnation. Trump stated that public opinion opposed broadcasting the execution, though Hasan will still be executed as scheduled. The cancellation reflects broader debate over the publicity and transparency surrounding capital punishment proceedings.
