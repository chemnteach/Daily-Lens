Topic 05 of 05
Trump Administration Declares Iran War Terminated Before Deadline
The Trump administration claims hostilities with Iran are "terminated" for War Powers Resolution purposes, citing an early April ceasefire agreement as the basis for this declaration ahead of Friday's 60-day congressional deadline. Under the War Powers Act, the president must either end military operations or secure congressional approval to continue them within 60 days of notifying Congress. The administration's statement appears designed to satisfy the legal requirement without requiring explicit congressional action. The timing comes as Senate Republicans have blocked Democratic efforts to mandate an end to the Iran operation, leaving the executive branch's interpretation of "terminated" hostilities as the determining factor in the dispute.
