Gilgo Beach Serial Killer Sentenced to Life Without Parole
Rex Heuermann, a 62-63-year-old Manhattan resident, has been sentenced to life in prison without parole for the Gilgo Beach serial killings spanning from 1993 to 2010. Heuermann pleaded guilty to seven murders and confessed to an eighth victim, with the sentencing taking place in Riverhead, New York on Wednesday. Families of the eight victims addressed the court during the proceeding. The case represents one of New York's most significant serial killer investigations, with the sentencing marking the conclusion of decades-long legal proceedings that began after the initial discovery of the crimes.
