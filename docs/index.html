Trump Pauses Army's Controversial Lead Azide Detonation in Iowa
The U.S. Army planned a controlled detonation of 68 barrels containing 9,500 pounds of unstable lead azide in Iowa, expected to release lead particles in a dust plume potentially extending 100 miles. President Trump intervened to pause the operation, citing concerns about the environmental and health risks. Meanwhile, Iowa's incoming governor and federal officials have debated responsibility for the final decision, with an appeals court declining to halt the detonation. Residents, lawmakers, and environmental groups criticized the lack of adequate public communication about the operation and its potential impacts.

FIFA Awards Women's World Cup Tournaments to UK and US
FIFA has confirmed major host assignments for upcoming Women's World Cup tournaments. The United Kingdom will host the 2035 tournament, marking the first time since 1966 that the country has hosted a World Cup. Meanwhile, the United States will co-host the 2031 Women's World Cup alongside Mexico, Costa Rica, and Jamaica.
