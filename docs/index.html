Trump Cancels Nearly $1 Billion in Congressional Funding
President Trump has canceled nearly $1 billion in federal spending approved by Congress, using a controversial power known as "pocket rescission" that allows him to bypass congressional approval. The cancellations primarily target Health and Human Services programs serving undocumented immigrants and diversity-focused initiatives. While the White House frames this as fiscal responsibility, critics highlight the rarity and contested nature of this executive power, which typically requires congressional authorization to revoke previously approved expenditures.
