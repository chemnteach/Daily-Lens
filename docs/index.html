Republicans Seek WNBA Investigation Into Caitlin Clark Treatment

While some sources frame the concern around possible racially motivated attacks and patterns similar to abuse experienced by Black players, others focus on broader questions about whether the league's treatment of Clark reflects institutional bias. The letter highlights hard fouls and physical play directed at the rookie, with conservatives questioning the league's response to these incidents.
