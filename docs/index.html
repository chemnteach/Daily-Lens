Maduro and Wife Indicted on Torture Conspiracy Charges
The Trump administration has unsealed a superseding indictment charging deposed Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife, Cilia Flores, with conspiracy to commit torture. The charges allege they ordered physical and psychological torture—including electric shock, asphyxiation, and sexual violence—against detainees, including U.S. citizens. Both have been detained in New York since early January. While sources uniformly report the indictment details and charges, left-leaning outlets emphasize the torture allegations themselves, center sources note the Trump administration's role in bringing charges, and right-leaning outlets focus on the specific methods alleged. The case represents the latest legal action against Maduro's government.

Trump Administration Suspends Microsoft, Adobe from Green Card Program
Vice President JD Vance announced Thursday that the Trump administration is suspending Microsoft and Adobe from the H-1B visa program, barring their workers from applying for permanent residency through green cards. The administration alleges the companies committed fraud in their use of the high-skilled worker visa program. The penalties represent a significant enforcement action against major technology firms over their employment practices. The announcement reflects the administration's stated focus on restricting immigration pathways for foreign workers in the tech sector.
