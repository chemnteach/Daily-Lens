SpaceX Starship Completes First Orbital Test Flight
SpaceX's Starship spacecraft successfully reached orbit for the first time on Monday, launching from Texas and completing a historic first orbital test flight. The vehicle splashed down in the Pacific Ocean north of Hawaii approximately three hours after liftoff, ending its flight earlier than the planned 10-hour mission that would have circled Earth six times. While the shortened duration fell short of SpaceX's objectives for testing the rocket's readiness for NASA's Artemis moon program, the achievement of reaching orbit represents a significant milestone in the development of the company's fully reusable spacecraft system.
