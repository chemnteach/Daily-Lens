Memphis Police Discover Remains of Three Young Children
Memphis police have discovered human remains believed to belong to three young children in a wooded area of Hickory Hill in southeast Memphis. The discovery began in March when a dog walker reported finding what appeared to be a human skull, prompting investigators to search the woodland location. Police indicate the remains had been in the area for several years and do not match any local missing persons reports. The investigation is ongoing as authorities work to identify the children and determine how they died.
