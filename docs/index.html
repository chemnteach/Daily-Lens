Supreme Court to Review Trump's Asylum Ban at Border
The Supreme Court agreed Friday to consider the constitutionality of Donald Trump's executive order that suspends asylum applications at the US-Mexico border, issued on Inauguration Day. Lower courts have previously ruled the ban illegal, and the Supreme Court will hear arguments this term with a decision expected by summer. The case represents a major test of the administration's immigration enforcement powers, with Trump officials seeking to overturn the lower court rulings blocking the asylum restrictions. The decision will determine whether the executive order can stand as a key component of the president's broader immigration crackdown.
