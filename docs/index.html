CDC Reports Second Measles Death as Outbreak Spreads
The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention announced a second measles death in the United States as the country faces a widening outbreak affecting multiple states. Pennsylvania has reported over 900 measles cases and four deaths associated with the outbreak, though the CDC's national tally currently reflects two confirmed deaths. A discrepancy exists between federal and state-level figures, with Pennsylvania's official count exceeding the CDC's documented cases. The outbreak continues to develop across at least 47 states, highlighting ongoing tensions between federal health agencies and state authorities regarding data collection and reporting.
