Appeals Court Orders Missouri to Use GOP Congressional Map
A federal appeals court has ruled that Missouri must use a new congressional redistricting map backed by Republicans and President Trump, despite the map being rejected twice by the Supreme Court. The map redraws Kansas City's congressional district, a change opponents argue affects voting representation. While the appeals court issued the order, implementation faces immediate practical obstacles, including that ballots have already been printed for the upcoming election. The ruling does not represent a final decision, as the case is expected to reach the Supreme Court for ultimate determination.
