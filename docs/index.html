Former Virginia Lt. Governor Dies in Murder-Suicide
Justin Fairfax, who served as Virginia's lieutenant governor from 2018 to 2022, killed his wife Cerina and then himself in an overnight incident at their Annandale home, according to Fairfax County Police.
