A federal appeals court has ruled that President Trump cannot proceed with construction of a $400 million White House ballroom without congressional approval. The ruling is expected to be appealed further, potentially reaching the Supreme Court. The case raises questions about executive power and the scope of presidential authority over White House grounds and facilities.
