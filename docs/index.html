Supreme Court Affirms Birthright Citizenship Constitutional Protection
The U.S. Supreme Court has reaffirmed the constitutional guarantee of birthright citizenship, upholding the precedent established in the landmark Wong Kim Ark case. The ruling means that individuals born in the United States are automatically granted citizenship regardless of their parents' immigration status. Supporters of the decision view it as a protection for all Americans and a reaffirmation of post-Civil War constitutional amendments, while the decision also represents a rejection of recent challenges to this longstanding principle. The case reflects ongoing constitutional debates about citizenship eligibility and immigration policy in the United States.
