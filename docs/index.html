ESPN Co-Founder Bill Rasmussen Dies at 93
Bill Rasmussen, the co-founder of ESPN, has died at age 93. Rasmussen conceived the initial idea for the sports network, which debuted in 1979 and fundamentally changed sports broadcasting. However, his tenure with the company proved brief—he was forced out after only one year when Getty Oil, the majority owner, took control. Despite his early departure from the network he founded, Rasmussen's vision established what became a dominant force in sports media.
