Trump Nominates Oklahoma Officer Schroyer as ICE Director
President Donald Trump announced Saturday that he will nominate Lance Schroyer, a former Oklahoma state trooper and longtime law enforcement officer, to serve as director of US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE).
