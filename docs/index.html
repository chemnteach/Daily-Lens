Nauru Officially Changes Name to Republic of Naoero
Nauru, the world's third-smallest country, has officially changed its name to the Republic of Naoero. The change reflects the nation's indigenous language and culture, with the country's international code shifting from NRU to NRO and its citizens now known as dei-Naoero rather than Nauruan. The government made the decision following extensive discussions among lawmakers, determining that a public referendum was unnecessary. The name change represents an assertion of national identity aligned with Nauru's native linguistic heritage.

Blanche Rescinds Trump's $1.8B Anti-Weaponization Fund
Acting Attorney General Todd Blanche formally terminated President Trump's $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization fund" via memo on Sunday night, a move that removes a major obstacle to his confirmation as attorney general. The fund had faced bipartisan criticism and was characterized by critics as a slush fund. While Blanche's rescission clears the path for GOP support of his nomination, reporting indicates the memo preserves a separate immunity order that shields Trump, his adult children, and their businesses from investigation for past conduct. The decision represents a compromise enabling his confirmation to proceed.
