Texas Appeals Court Allows Ten Commandments in Schools
A federal appeals court has ruled that Texas can require public schools to display the Ten Commandments in classrooms. The 5th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals issued a narrow 9-8 decision reversing a lower court's previous block on the law. The ruling represents a significant victory for conservative advocates seeking greater religious expression in public education. The decision opens questions about the constitutionality of mixing religious content with public institutions, an issue likely to face further legal challenges.
