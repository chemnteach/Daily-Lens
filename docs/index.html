Supreme Court Blocks Trump's Trans Prison Care Restrictions
The Supreme Court declined to enforce the Trump administration's policy restricting gender-affirming care for transgender inmates, allowing a lower court's block to remain in place. The administration sought to halt hormone treatments and certain surgeries for incarcerated transgender individuals, but the Court refused to lift the injunction against the policy. Conservative Justices Thomas and Alito dissented from the decision, indicating a split within the Court on the issue. The ruling represents a setback for the administration's attempted restrictions on transgender medical care in federal prisons.
