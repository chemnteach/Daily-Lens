Federal Appeals Court Blocks Mail-Order Abortion Pill Access
A three-judge panel of the 5th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals ruled Friday that mifepristone, an FDA-approved abortion pill, can no longer be dispensed through mail-order or prescribed via telehealth. The court sided with Louisiana in a lawsuit challenging FDA regulations that had permitted remote access to the medication. The ruling restricts abortion access nationwide by requiring in-person clinic distribution only. The decision represents a significant legal challenge to established FDA policy and is expected to face further litigation.
