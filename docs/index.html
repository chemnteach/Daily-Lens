Democrats Reshape 2028 Primary Calendar With South Carolina First
The Democratic National Committee has formally approved a revised 2028 primary calendar that makes South Carolina the leadoff state on January 22, 2028, replacing Iowa's traditional top position. Nevada follows on February 1, marking a significant restructuring of the primary schedule. The change emphasizes voting power for Black and Latino voters, as well as union members who comprise larger portions of South Carolina's and Nevada's Democratic electorates compared to Iowa. This represents a deliberate shift in which early primary contests may influence the party's presidential nomination process.
