Justice Department Activates Dormant Alien Terrorist Removal Court
The Department of Justice is bringing the first case in the Alien Terrorist Removal Court, a specialized tribunal created by Congress in 1996 but never previously used. The court allows prosecutors to use classified evidence in deportation proceedings against noncitizens suspected of terrorism connections. The DOJ is seeking to remove Nazira Haji Zada, a 47-year-old Afghan woman in Texas, citing her sons' terrorism convictions. The case marks a significant activation of a legal mechanism designed to streamline national security-related deportations while maintaining confidentiality of sensitive intelligence.
