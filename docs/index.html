President Trump has terminated the remaining members of the Election Assistance Commission, an independent federal body that assists election administration officials across the country. Left-leaning sources emphasize concerns about potential election "chaos" and describe the commission as bipartisan, while centrist outlets focus on the factual removal of Democratic members. The commission maintains responsibilities including overseeing mail-voter registration forms and other election administration duties.
