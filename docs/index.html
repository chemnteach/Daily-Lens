Algae Bloom Clouds Washington's Reflecting Pool
The Reflecting Pool in Washington, D.C. has become clouded with algae, drawing attention to the monument's condition. The shallow, sunny waters create ideal conditions for algae growth during summer months, and experts suggest the recent renovation may have accelerated the problem. While the pool was intended to attract visitors and serve as a centerpiece, it currently displays murky water, peeling paint, and visible debris rather than the pristine appearance officials sought.

Weight-Loss Drugs Show Unexpected Health Benefits Beyond Weight Loss
Recent research reveals that GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic and Wegovy, taken by approximately one-in-eight American adults, may provide health benefits beyond their primary weight-loss function. Studies suggest these medications could help reduce cancer risk or treat certain cancers, while other research indicates they may boost male fertility in obese men without suppressing male hormones. These findings represent the latest in a growing body of unexpected health advantages being associated with this class of drugs, expanding their potential clinical applications.
