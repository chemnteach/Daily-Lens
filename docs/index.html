New York Sues Prediction Market Polymarket Over Gambling
New York has filed a lawsuit against Polymarket, a prediction market platform, alleging it operates as an unlicensed gambling service that poses risks to state residents. The action represents the latest in a series of similar enforcement efforts by New York against crypto and prediction market platforms, including Kalshi, Coinbase, and Gemini. The platforms argue they operate under federal regulation by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission and maintain that states lack authority to govern their activities. The lawsuit raises ongoing questions about regulatory jurisdiction and whether prediction markets should be classified as gambling or federally-regulated financial instruments.
