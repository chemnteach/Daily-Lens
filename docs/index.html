Trump Orders Pentagon Switch to Steam Catapults on Carriers
President Trump has ordered the Pentagon to redesign U.S. aircraft carriers to use steam catapults instead of the current electromagnetic launch systems. Trump has repeatedly criticized the modern electromagnetic systems since 2017, calling them overly complicated. Democratic Senator Mark Kelly has opposed the directive, arguing it would be costly and questioning Trump's expertise in military technology decisions. The order represents a reversal to older catapult technology on carriers that had transitioned to the newer systems in recent years.
