Supreme Court Considers Mail-In Ballot Counting Rules
The Supreme Court is hearing a case challenging how states count mail-in ballots received after Election Day. The Republican National Committee seeks to overturn procedures in 29 states that currently accept ballots postmarked by Election Day but arriving afterward. The case could significantly affect how millions of votes are counted in upcoming elections. Left-leaning sources present this as a procedural challenge to existing state practices, while right-leaning sources frame it as an effort to tighten voting deadlines. The Court's decision will determine whether states can maintain their current mail-ballot timelines or must count only ballots physically received by Election Day.
