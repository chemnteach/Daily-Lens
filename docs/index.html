CNN Founder Ted Turner Dies at Age 87
Ted Turner, the media mogul who founded CNN in 1980 and revolutionized cable news, has died at age 87. Turner began his career in advertising before launching the country's first 24-hour all-news network, which transformed television journalism and established him as a major figure in broadcasting history. Sources across the political spectrum acknowledge his significant impact on the media landscape, with coverage emphasizing both his larger-than-life personality and his pioneering role in reshaping how news is delivered. The obituaries reflect broad recognition of Turner's influence on cable television and journalism.

U.S. Gasoline Prices Surge Amid Iran Conflict
U.S. gasoline prices have risen approximately 50 percent since military conflict with Iran began, with the national average reaching $4.53 per gallon according to AAA data. The price increases are primarily attributed to crude oil climbing due to Iran's closure of the Strait of Hormuz, a critical global energy waterway. Recent weekly gains have added 31 cents to pump prices. While sources agree on the fundamental connection between the Iran conflict and rising fuel costs through disrupted oil supplies, the exact mechanisms and broader implications remain subjects of ongoing analysis.
