Alex Ovechkin Announces Retirement After 22 NHL Seasons

Supreme Court Examines Climate Liability Case Against Energy Companies
The Supreme Court began its new term with oral arguments in Suncor v. Boulder, a major case determining whether state and local governments can pursue climate damage lawsuits against fossil fuel companies in state courts. The case centers on whether Boulder County's suit against Suncor and ExxonMobil should proceed in state or federal court, with implications for future climate litigation efforts. The dispute involves questions about federal authority over environmental matters and the proper venue for climate-related damages claims. The outcome could significantly shape how states and municipalities pursue legal remedies against energy firms for climate-related harms.
