FDA Corrects Cyclospora Test Result in Taylor Farms Lettuce
The FDA announced Sunday that laboratory re-examination determined a cyclospora finding in lettuce from Taylor Farms was a false positive. The initial positive result, reported Saturday, had raised concerns amid an ongoing nationwide cyclospora outbreak causing diarrheal illness. Taylor Farms confirmed the company was informed of the FDA's error. The correction clarifies that the sample does not indicate contamination, though a broader cyclospora outbreak continues affecting multiple regions across the United States.

The price increase reflects regional instability, though it varies significantly by state, with some drivers already paying well above the national average. Energy analysts link the price movement directly to the renewed conflict and its impact on global oil markets.
