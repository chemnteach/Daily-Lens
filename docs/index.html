Death Row Prisoner Released on Bond After Conviction Overturned
Richard Glossip, 63, has been released on bond after spending nearly 30 years in custody since his 1997 arrest for murder. The U.S. Supreme Court overturned his conviction last year, paving the way for the Oklahoma judge's decision to grant bond while he awaits retrial. Glossip came close to execution on three separate occasions during his incarceration. The case represents a significant development in his legal proceedings, marking the first time he has been freed since his initial arrest nearly three decades ago.
