Federal Judge Blocks ICE Arrests at Immigration Courthouses
A federal judge has blocked the Trump administration from making immigration arrests at courthouses nationwide, marking the second legal setback for the practice in recent months. U.S. District Judge Casey Pitts issued the ruling Tuesday, finding that Immigration and Customs Enforcement's allowance of arrests at immigration courthouses presents constitutional concerns. This follows a May decision by a New York judge that similarly barred courthouse arrests at immigration courts in that jurisdiction. The rulings reflect ongoing judicial scrutiny of immigration enforcement tactics during court proceedings.
