Spirit Airlines Ceases Operations After 34 Years
Spirit Airlines has shut down all operations immediately after failing to secure a government bailout, leaving thousands of passengers stranded and employees without jobs. The Trump administration had drafted a $500 million rescue plan, though some Congressional allies opposed it. Political figures have blamed different factors for the collapse: Republicans point to Biden-era policies blocking a JetBlue merger, while Democrats cite fuel price increases linked to regional tensions. The closure marks the end of the discount carrier's 34-year history amid broader challenges facing the airline industry.
