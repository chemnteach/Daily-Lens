North Korea Linked to Record $6.3B Binance Cryptocurrency Hack
Blockchain intelligence firms have attributed a $6.3 billion hack of Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, to a North Korean cybercrime group. Two major blockchain analytics companies, TRM Labs and Chainalysis, have tied the attack to TraderTraitor, a known North Korean hacking unit with a history of cyber operations. The hack represents the largest cryptocurrency theft on record. The incident highlights ongoing cybersecurity vulnerabilities in the crypto industry and the involvement of state-linked actors in financial crimes targeting digital assets.
